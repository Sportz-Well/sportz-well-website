For academies and schools using SWPI · Last updated 24 October 2026

Between Sportz-Well, 502, Vinca 2, Cosmos Park, Vijay Garden Road, Ghodbunder Road, Thane West 400 607, Maharashtra (“Sportz-Well”) and the academy or school named in the order form (“the Academy”). Prices are not in these terms: they are in the order form or quote the Academy accepts.

1. The service

SWPI is an app for the Academy’s coaches. It records players, attendance, coach assessments and matches, and produces monthly, video and yearly reports for players and parents. Reports are coaching aids. They are not medical advice, injury predictions or selection decisions.

2. Accounts

The Academy chooses who gets an account, keeps passwords private, and tells Sportz-Well when someone should lose access. Head coaches and directors manage the player list; assistant coaches record assessments only.

3. What the Academy does

  1. Enters player details accurately, including batting hand and bowling type. The reports treat these as fact and never guess them from video.
  2. Gets a parent’s or guardian’s recorded consent, through SWPI’s consent form, before a player is filmed or analysed.
  3. Films safely: the camera person always stays outside the nets.
  4. Tells Sportz-Well when a player leaves, so their data can be deleted on time.

4. What Sportz-Well does

  1. Delivers the reports in the plan the Academy chose.
  2. Keeps the Academy’s data separate from every other academy’s, protected and backed up.
  3. Uses the data only to provide the service, on the Academy’s instructions, and never sells it or uses it for advertising.
  4. Helps the Academy answer parents’ requests to see, correct or delete their child’s data.
  5. Tells the Academy without delay if its data is exposed.

5. AI in the reports

Reports use AI: Google’s Gemini AI watches practice videos, and an AI assistant helps write report text. AI can be wrong, so a person reviews every report before it is shared, and the Academy’s coaches have the final say on their players. Workload or injury-related notes always advise speaking to a physiotherapist.

6. Fees

The fees, plan length and number of players are in the order form or quote, plus 18% GST, paid in advance for the plan period. No refund is given for a plan period that has already started.

7. Who owns the data

The Academy’s player data belongs to the Academy. Sportz-Well holds it only to provide the service.

8. Names and logos

Neither side uses the other’s name, logo or players’ images in marketing without written permission.

9. Ending the service

Either side can end the service with 30 days’ written notice, or at once if the other side seriously breaks these terms. On request within 30 days of the end, Sportz-Well sends the Academy copies of its reports. After that, the data is deleted within 30 days; backups clear within 90 days.

10. Availability

Sportz-Well works to keep SWPI running and gives notice of planned maintenance, but cannot promise it will never be interrupted.

11. Limits

Sportz-Well’s total liability is limited to the fees the Academy paid in the 12 months before the claim. Neither side is liable for indirect losses. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

12. Changes

Sportz-Well gives 30 days’ written notice of any change to these terms. A change does not take effect for the current paid period without the Academy’s agreement.

13. Law and disputes
