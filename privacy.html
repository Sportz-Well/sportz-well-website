Who we are

Sportz-Well, 502, Vinca 2, Cosmos Park, Vijay Garden Road, Ghodbunder Road, Thane West 400 607, Maharashtra runs SWPI (Sportz-Well Performance Intelligence), a service that helps cricket academies and schools track and report on their players’ development. Questions: privacy@sportz-well.com.

Children’s data

We analyse a child’s data only after a parent or legal guardian has given recorded consent, and the app blocks analysis without it. We do not use children’s data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not build profiles for anyone outside the child’s academy. The parent consent notice, shown on the consent form, is the full description.

Why we use data

To produce the reports the academy subscribes to, to let coaches record and review their players’ development, to keep accounts secure, and to answer requests. Nothing else.

Who processes it, and where

Render (database), Cloudinary (videos and photos), Google (Gemini AI analysis and email), Vercel (hosts the website and app pages) and GitHub (encrypted backups). They act on our instructions, on paid accounts, with servers outside India. Under Google’s paid-service terms, data sent to the Gemini AI is not used to improve Google’s products.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets one cookie, which keeps a coach signed in. Neither the website nor the app uses advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep data

Player and parent data: while the player is enrolled, then deleted within 30 days of leaving or of a parent’s request; encrypted backups clear within 90 days. Coach accounts: until the academy closes the account, then deleted within 30 days.

How we protect it

Each academy’s records are separated from every other academy’s on every database request. Contact numbers are encrypted at rest; passwords are stored only in a protected (hashed) form; administrator sign-in needs a one-time code; nightly backups are encrypted.

Your rights

You can ask for a summary of your data, correction, completion or deletion; withdraw consent; and name a nominee. Parents can also go through their child’s academy. Write to privacy@sportz-well.com; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If something goes wrong

If personal data is exposed, we tell the affected academy, the affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Changes

We will post any change here with a new date. A material change to how children’s data is used needs fresh parental consent.
